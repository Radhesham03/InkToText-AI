How it works

Clean up

OpenCV removes noise and shadows, boosts contrast and erases ruled lines.

Find lines

Each line of your page is located and cut out, keeping paragraph gaps.

Read

The TrOCR handwriting model reads every line and reports how sure it is.

Limitations: handwriting recognition is never perfect. Messy writing, faint ink, crossed-out words, heavy skew and non-English text lower accuracy. Always proofread the result. Lines marked low confidence need extra attention. This model reads English only.
